About Our Hospital

SR. Christinem Memorial Hospital is dedicated to serving our community and helping patients access healthcare services. We value compassion, respect and affordable care.

Our location is along Masaba–Lukhome Road, approximately 400 metres from the tarmac.

  • Open 24 hours a day
  • Affordable healthcare services
  • Convenient road access
  • Telephone booking and enquiries
